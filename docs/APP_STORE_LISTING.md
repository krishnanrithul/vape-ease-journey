## Privacy Policy

_Last updated September 25, 2026_

**The short version:** VapeWise has no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you log stays on your device, in your device's own local storage. We never see it, because there's nowhere for it to go — the app has no backend to send it to.

### What VapeWise stores

VapeWise records the information you enter to track your vaping habits and progress: puff counts and timestamps, your daily goal and baseline, streak and achievement progress, and your app preferences (theme, reminder settings). All of it is saved using your device's built-in local storage — the same mechanism any app on your phone uses to remember its own settings.

### What we don't do

- We don't collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. There is no server for it to go to.
- We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs of any kind.
- We don't require an account, an email address, or any sign-in to use the app.
- We don't share, sell, or otherwise disclose your information, because it never reaches us in the first place.

### Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, VapeWise schedules a local notification directly through your device's operating system. This happens entirely on-device — no reminder content or schedule is ever sent anywhere.

### Your data, your control

You can export a full backup of your data as a file at any time from Settings, and import it back in later — useful if you're moving to a new device. You can also permanently delete all app data from Settings, or simply delete the app itself, which removes everything VapeWise has stored.

### Children's privacy

VapeWise is a harm-reduction tool built around tracking and reducing vaping, and is intended for adult users who already vape. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, since the app collects nothing at all.

### Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for instance, if a future version of the app adds an optional feature that involves data leaving your device — the hosted page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date.

**Description**

VapeWise is a private, judgment-free way to track your vaping and cut down gradually instead of cold-turkey.

**Track without judgment**
Log puffs in one tap. No accounts, no cloud sync, no one watching — just a clear picture of your own habits, building over time.

**Reduce at your own pace**
Set a daily goal based on your actual baseline, not a generic number. VapeWise nudges your target down gradually as you build consistency, so progress feels achievable instead of all-or-nothing.

**Build real streaks**
Every under-goal day counts toward a streak. Milestones and achievements celebrate real progress — not just showing up, but actually cutting down.

**Ride out a craving**
A built-in breathing/delay timer helps you get through a craving without reaching for a vape, right when you need it.

**Your data never leaves your phone**
Everything — puff logs, goals, streaks — is stored locally on your device. No account required, nothing uploaded anywhere. Export a backup any time you want one.

**Gentle reminders**
Optional daily reminders keep you checking in, without being naggy about it.

VapeWise isn't a substitute for medical advice or a cessation program. It's a tracking and awareness tool for people who've decided to reduce on their own terms.
